## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: small floating help assistant avatar for a rabbit show app. Create a photorealistic domestic rabbit portrait cutout on a genuinely transparent background with alpha. One natural chestnut agouti rabbit, facing camera with a slight curious head tilt, both upright ears fully visible, alert kind dark eyes, realistic fine fur, whiskers and natural rabbit anatomy. Head and upper chest only, centered, filling square canvas with tight but safe margins around ear tips. Soft studio lighting, crisp silhouette legible at small icon size. No cartoon styling, no clothes, accessories, lettering, border, floor, background scene or shadow rectangle. Output one square transparent PNG asset.
